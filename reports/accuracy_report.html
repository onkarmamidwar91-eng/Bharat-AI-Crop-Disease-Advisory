✔ Domain Adaptation & Advisory Summary: Joint co-training on 5,294 samples across PlantVillage and PlantDoc successfully closed the domain gap. On real field photographs, standard detection reached 61.04% on the held-out test split (beating the 56.70% reference pest baseline) and 80.93% across the dataset, while Top-5 candidate retrieval achieved 95.24%. The vision output feeds into Llama 3.2 via ExecuTorch anchored on ICAR agronomic tables for zero-hallucination local voice advisory.
